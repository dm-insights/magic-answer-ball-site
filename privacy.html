Privacy Policy

Magic Answer Ball: Shake & Ask · Effective 20 November 2026

In short: the app collects no data. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.
  • It has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • It does not use the internet: the Android app does not even ask for network access.
  • It does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

What stays on your phone

The balls you create, their answers and your settings (theme, sound, vibration) are saved only on your phone, inside the app. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

If you have turned on your phone's own backup (Google backup on Android, iCloud on iPhone), your phone may include these app settings in that backup. That backup is run by Google or Apple under their privacy terms; we never receive or see it.

Motion sensor

The app reads the phone's motion sensor only while it is open, to notice when you shake the phone. These readings are used on the spot and are never saved or sent anywhere.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this (for example, if ads are ever added), we will update this page and the store listing before that version is released.
