How it works

  1. Think of a question — anything with a yes or no.
  2. Shake your phone, tap the ball or give it a flick.
  3. Feel it tumble, slow down and turn to show your answer.

Support

Shaking does nothing.

Shake the phone briskly, or simply tap the ball.

No sound.

Check that Sound is on in the app's Settings and that your phone is not in silent mode.

No vibration.

Check Vibration in the app's Settings and your phone's touch feedback setting.

Where are my balls saved?

Only on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Is it real fortune-telling?

No. Answers are random and the app is for entertainment only. But it's surprisingly good at helping you decide.
